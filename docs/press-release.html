A shop-ready bike service report in 60 seconds

Use the ride data you already have. Get a bike maintenance score, a plain-language wear report, and a work order your local bike shop can act on — before you're stranded on the side of the road with a flat tire.

The problem

Strava knows your miles, but it doesn't know the state of your bike. It tracks your rides but doesn't monitor component wear. The mileage data is right there: riders can see 3,432 miles on a bike. But Strava never warns you that your brake pads are rubbing away with only a few miles left.

Strava is a social product, with little focus on a huge part of the rider experience: bike maintenance. On top of that, fitness enthusiasts have subscription fatigue. Nobody wants yet another subscription just to know when to take their bike to the shop. We're changing that with a lightweight app, and enabling local bike shops to win while we do it.

The solution

1

Drop in your Strava data

No manual data entry. Connect your account or drop in the export you already have.

2

Answer a few quick questions

What kind of bike, what conditions you ride in, and, if you know, when the chain or tires were last replaced.

3

Get your report

An overall health score and a card for every wearing component, sorted by urgency, with the mileage evidence behind each.

Wear estimates adjust to your actual mileage and riding conditions. Wet-weather riding shortens component life, and the report knows it.

Why local bike shops win

The report's final step is the differentiator. Riders find nearby bike shops in the app and generate a one-click Shop Work Order — a printable checklist for the mechanic, grouping what's likely due now and what to keep an eye on.

Customers walk in knowing what they need, and the mechanic gets a digestible list of the bike's status instead of a vague "can you look it over?"

What's next

  • Native iOS app with HealthKit and background sync. Reports stay current automatically, with notifications when work is overdue. No manual sync step at all.
  • Garmin and Wahoo export support. Not all riders use Strava, and we'll meet them where they are.
  • Shop-side view. Shops receive work orders ahead of the visit and make sure the parts are in stock before the bike arrives.
