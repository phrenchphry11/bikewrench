# Bike Health Report: a shop-ready service report in 60 seconds

**Use the ride data you already have. Get a bike maintenance score, a plain-language wear report, and a work order that your local bike shop can act on before you're stranded on the side of the road with a flat tire.**

## The problem

Strava knows your miles, but it doesn't know the state of your bike. It tracks your rides but doesn't monitor component wear.  The mileage data is right there - riders can see 3,432 miles on a bike - but Strava never warns you that your brake pads are rubbing away and only have a few miles left. Strava's a social product, but has little focus on a huge part of rider experience - bike maintenance.  On top of that, fitness enthusiasts have subscription fatigue, and they don't want yet another subscription just so they can track when they need to take their bike to the shop.  

We're changing that by building a lightweight app and enabling local bike shops to win while doing it.

## The solution

Bike Health Report turns that existing ride data into answers in three steps:

1. **Drop in your Strava data** — No manual data entry needed.  Just use data you already have.
2. **Answer a few quick questions** — What kind of bike, what conditions you ride in, and (if you happen to know) when the chain or tires were last replaced.
3. **Get your report** — Get an overall health score and a card for every wearing component, sorted by urgency, each with the mileage evidence and a plain-language reason why it matters.

Wear estimates are adjusted for your actual mileage and riding conditions — wet-weather riding shortens component life, and the report knows it.

## Why local bike shops win

The report's final step is the differentiator.  We allow users to find local bike shops and offer a one-click **Shop Work Order** — a printable checklist to share to the mechanic, grouping what's likely due now and what to keep an eye on. Customers walk in knowing what they need, and we save the bike mechanic time by giving them a digestible list of the bike's status.

## What's next

- **Native iOS app with HealthKit and background sync.** Your report stays current automatically (and sends notifications when work is overdue).  Native app users can skip the manual sync step entirely.
- **Garmin and Wahoo export support.**  Not all riders use Strava, and we'll support that.
- **Shop-side view** — Let a shop receive work orders ahead of the visit and make sure they have the necessary parts in stock.
